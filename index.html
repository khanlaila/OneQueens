Just Arrived? Start Here

  • Get an ID / CityKey
    NYC ID card available to all residents regardless of status. Visit a enrollment center.
  • Open a Bank Account
    Many banks accept ITIN or foreign ID. Capital One and Chase have branches throughout Queens.
  • Enroll Kids in School
    All children have the right to attend NYC public schools. Visit your local Family Welcome Center.
  • Find a Doctor
    NYC H+H hospitals serve everyone regardless of insurance or immigration status. Apply for NYC Care.
  • Get a MetroCard / OMNY
    Tap your phone or buy a card at any subway station. Reduced fare available for qualifying residents.
  • Apply for an ITIN
    Individual Taxpayer Identification Number lets you file taxes and open bank accounts. Visit a VITA site for free help.

Know Your Rights

If ICE Comes to Your Door

You have the right to not open the door. Ask to see a warrant signed by a judge. You have the right to remain silent and to a lawyer.

ACLUF: 212-549-2500

Tenant Rights

Landlords cannot evict you without a court order. You have the right to heat, hot water, and safe conditions. Report violations to 311.

Met Council: 212-693-0553

Workers' Rights

All workers have rights regardless of immigration status: minimum wage, safe conditions, and the right to report violations without retaliation.

DOL: 888-4-NYSDOL

Getting Around Queens

🚋

Subway

E, F, 7, N, W, R lines connect major Queens hubs. $2.90 per ride.

🚍

Bus

Extensive bus network. Q44, Q58, Q60 are key crosstown routes. Free transfers.

📱

OMNY

Tap phone or card at any reader. No MetroCard needed. Install the OMNY app.

🚕

Citi Bike

Bike share in western Queens. $4.49/single ride. E-bikes available.
