Know Your Rights as a Tenant

Right to Safe Housing

Landlords must provide heat, hot water, and safe conditions. If they don't, you can withhold rent or call 311.

311

No Illegal Eviction

Landlords cannot lock you out, shut off utilities, or remove your belongings without a court order.

NYC HRA: 718-557-1399

Rent Stabilization

Many Queens apartments are rent-stabilized. Check if yours is at the NYC Rent Guidelines Board website.

Rent Board: 212-385-2934

Housing Checklist for Newcomers

  • 📝
    Know Your Lease Rights
    Read your lease carefully. All NYC tenants have rights regardless of immigration status.
  • 📲
    Document Everything
    Take photos of your apartment condition. Save all communications with your landlord.
  • 🗓
    Apply for Housing Lottery
    NYC Housing Connect offers affordable units. Create a profile at housingconnect.nyc.gov
  • ✅
    Get Renters Insurance
    Protect your belongings. Many plans cost under $15/month.
