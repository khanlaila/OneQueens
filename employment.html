Know Your Rights at Work

Minimum Wage

All workers must be paid at least $16/hour in NYC. This applies regardless of immigration status. Tips cannot be taken by employers.

DOL: 888-4-NYSDOL

Safe Workplace

You have the right to a safe workplace. Report dangerous conditions without fear of retaliation. OSHA protections apply to everyone.

OSHA: 800-321-6742

No Wage Theft

Unpaid wages, off-the-clock work, and denied breaks are illegal. You can file a claim up to 6 years back. Status does not matter.

DOL: 212-775-3880

Getting Job-Ready

  • 📄
    Prepare Your Resume
    Include work history from your home country. Queens libraries offer free resume help.
  • 🏅
    Get Credentials Evaluated
    Foreign degrees may need evaluation. WES and ECE provide this service for a fee.
  • 💻
    Create LinkedIn Profile
    Many employers use LinkedIn. Queens Public Library offers free workshops.
  • 📝
    Apply for ITIN if Needed
    Individual Taxpayer ID Number lets you work legally and file taxes. Free help at VITA sites.

Quick Job Search Tips

💼

Visit Workforce1

Free city-run job centers. Resume help, interview prep, and job listings.

📲

Use Job Apps

Indeed, LinkedIn, and Snagajob are popular. Many list no-experience jobs.

🌟

Network

Ask community members. Many jobs in Queens are found through word-of-mouth.

📚

Learn English

ESL classes improve job prospects. Many are free at Queens Public Library.
