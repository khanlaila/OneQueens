Healthcare & Wellness

Access medical care in Queens regardless of insurance or immigration status. Everyone has the right to treatment.

✅ You can see a doctor. Your immigration status does not matter.
NYC law requires hospitals to treat all patients regardless of ability to pay or immigration status. Health information is kept private and is not shared with immigration enforcement.

What You Need to Know

💳

No Insurance Needed

NYC H+H hospitals and many clinics treat uninsured patients. Ask about sliding-scale fees based on income.

🔒

Your Info Is Private

Medical records are protected by law. Hospitals cannot share your information with ICE or immigration authorities.

💬

Free Interpreters

All NYC hospitals provide free interpretation in 200+ languages. Just tell the front desk your language.

🏠

Emergency = Always Free

Emergency rooms must treat you regardless of status. Call 911 for emergencies — ambulances will come to anyone.

Insurance & Coverage Options

🚍

NYC Care

Uninsured, any status

Low-cost or free healthcare through NYC H+H. Covers doctor visits, prescriptions, mental health, and specialty care. Enroll at any H+H location.

💉

Medicaid

Low-income, certain statuses

Free health insurance for qualifying low-income residents. Emergency Medicaid available regardless of immigration status. Apply through NY State of Health.

👶

Child Health Plus

Children under 19, any status

Free or low-cost health insurance for children regardless of immigration status. Covers check-ups, immunizations, dental, vision, and prescriptions.

🚨

Emergency Medicaid

Anyone with emergency condition

Covers emergency room visits, labor & delivery, and emergency surgery for undocumented immigrants. Apply at the hospital during your visit.

Any ID you have (passport, NYC ID). Proof of address if possible. Don't worry if you don't have documents.

Follow Up

Keep your appointment card. Ask about referrals for specialists, prescriptions, and ongoing care.

Clinics & Hospitals

Common Questions

Can I see a doctor if I'm undocumented? ▼
Yes. NYC public hospitals (NYC Health + Hospitals) treat all patients regardless of immigration status or ability to pay. You can also enroll in NYC Care for ongoing low-cost or free healthcare. Your information is kept confidential.
Will the hospital report me to immigration? ▼
No. NYC law (Executive Order 41) prohibits city employees, including hospital staff, from asking about or sharing your immigration status. Your medical information is protected by federal privacy laws (HIPAA).
What if I can't afford to pay? ▼
NYC H+H hospitals offer a sliding-scale fee based on your income. If your income is very low, care may be free. Ask the financial counselor at the hospital about "H+H Options" or NYC Care enrollment. You will never be turned away for inability to pay.
How do I get prescription medication? ▼
NYC Care and Medicaid cover prescriptions. If uninsured, ask your doctor about generic alternatives. Many pharmacies at H+H hospitals offer steeply discounted medications. Community health centers often have on-site pharmacies with sliding-scale pricing.
Where can I get vaccinated? ▼
Free vaccinations (flu, COVID-19, childhood immunizations) are available at NYC H+H hospitals, community health centers listed below, and through the NYC Department of Health walk-in immunization clinics. No ID or insurance required at most locations.
